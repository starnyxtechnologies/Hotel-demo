A LITTLE ABOUT DEMO HOTEL

Rooted in flavour.
Made for togetherness.

Demo Hotel is a sample restaurant brand created to demonstrate a complete restaurant website experience. Its story celebrates the familiar comfort of Indian meals, the joy of sharing a table and the flavours that remind us of home.

The sample menu brings together Maharashtrian favourites, vegetarian classics, tandoor-inspired plates and comforting desserts — all presented in a warm, family-friendly setting.

For the real restaurant, this story, menu, photographs and details can be tailored to reflect its own kitchen and people.
